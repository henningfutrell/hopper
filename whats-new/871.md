- Infrastructure changes made outside of git are now tracked. When a job finds drift, or a change by a person or tool that is not GitOps and not an approved plan, you get a notification with a link, and the change shows on the new **Infra changes** page in the main menu, with a count of changes waiting for you. Mark each one as adopted, reverted, or accepted with a reason. Plans waiting for approval are on the same page.
