- Infrastructure changes made outside of git are now tracked. When a job finds drift, or a change by a person or tool that is not GitOps and not an approved plan, you get a notification, and the change shows in Settings, Vault, Out-of-band changes. Mark each one as adopted, reverted, or accepted with a reason.
- A check that runs outside the hopper, on a schedule of your own, can now report infrastructure changes made outside of git, with an API token. They show and notify the same way.
- A ready-made check can now look for infrastructure changes made outside of git on a schedule of your own: Argo CD and Flux sync, Terraform plans, CloudTrail and the Kubernetes audit log. The deploy guide says how to set it up.
