- An admin can turn on model routing: simple work runs on a cheaper model and complex or risky work on the stronger one, each with its own spending budget. When a job on the cheaper model fails, its next try runs on the stronger model. The job page shows which model a job got, why, and its budget against what it spent.
- A person can set a spending budget on one job from its job page. When a job on the cheaper model reaches its budget and a person lets it go on, it goes on on the stronger model. An admin can now change model routing in Settings.
