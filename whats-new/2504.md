- A job's page now shows what the hopper made for the job on its machine, and whether each thing was removed, kept for work not pushed, or could not be removed.
- When a finished job's cleanup leaves something on a machine for more than an hour, Failures now lists the job under Stuck, and the event list says what is still there.
- When a machine is removed, what an ended job left on it no longer stays on Failures for good: the job page says the machine is gone and nothing there was removed.
