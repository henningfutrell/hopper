- Each job's page now shows where its change is on its way to production and what it waits on, and once it is done, where its work ended up.
- On a job's page you can now say whether its change is to be deployed, and change your mind until it is deployed. Once it is deployed, that answer is final.
- A job's page now lists each edit of its issue's text while the job was on its way, and at which step.
- For changes to the hopper itself, a job's page now shows when its change reached the early release and the stable release, and which commit carried it.
- The jobs list now shows, for each job whose change is not delivered yet, the step its change is in and what it waits on.
- On a job's page you can now hold a job's change before it is merged or released, or take it over and do that step yourself, and then let it go on.
- When a job runs again, its page now says that its change moved to the new job, with a link to it.
