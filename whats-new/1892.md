- Intake now shows where the hopper's own records say each issue stands, even when the labels on the issue are old.
- When you take the hopper's blocked or waiting label off an issue by hand, the hopper runs that issue again.
- When an issue waits on your answer and an old label of the hopper stayed on it, the hopper now takes the old label off.
- When you put one of the hopper's end labels, for example its rejected label, on a request in Intake by hand, the request leaves Intake and the label stays.
- When an issue waits on you after its job failed, Intake shows it as failed even when its labels are old, and the hopper corrects the labels.
